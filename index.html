About CivicOS Labs

A Florida LLC building civic infrastructure from the ground up.

Who We Are

CivicOS Labs, LLC is a civic technology company based in West Palm Beach, Florida. We build open tools that make local government transparent, accountable, and accessible.

Founded in 2026 with a clear mission: every citizen should be able to understand what their government is buying, who's selling it, and whether it works.

How We Operate

We earn revenue through deployments, audits, and data services — not by selling citizen data or locking governments into proprietary platforms.

CivicOS Labs is structured to support a companion 501(c)(3) nonprofit for grant-funded research and educational programs. Mission and market, aligned.
